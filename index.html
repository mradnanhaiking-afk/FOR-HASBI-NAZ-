Theek hai, to kehna ye hai ki hum dono ke beech jo bhi galtiyan hoti hain, un sab ke liye main maafi maangta hoon. Kabhi-kabhi main tum par itna chillata hoon, itna ladta hoon, aur mujhe khud samajh nahi aata ki main kya keh raha hoon. Lekin iska matlab ye nahi ki meri galti sahi ho jaati hai. Mujhe apne gusse aur apne lafzon ko behtar banana hai.

Main tumhe apni zindagi ka ek bahut khaas hissa maanta hoon aur aage ke safar mein tumhara saath chahta hoon. Mujhe umeed hai ki hum ek doosre ko samjhenge, ek doosre ki baat sunenge aur har mushkil ko milkar suljhane ki koshish karenge.

Kabhi tumhari kuch baatein mujhe bahut hurt kar deti hain aur kabhi meri baatein tumhe. Main chahta hoon ki hum dono ek doosre ke emotions ki kadar karein. Main apni feelings tumse honestly share karna chahta hoon, lekin tumhari freedom, tumhare dreams aur tumhari khushi bhi mere liye utni hi important hain.

Kabhi-kabhi mujhe jealousy bhi hoti hai, aur shayad main apni feelings ko theek se express nahi kar pata. Lekin main ye bhi samajhta hoon ki pyaar sirf expectations ka naam nahi hai. Pyaar mein trust, respect aur ek doosre ko samajhna bhi zaroori hai.

Main chahta hoon ki humare beech itni understanding ho ki hum bina dare apni har baat keh sakein. Narazgi ho to baat karke door karein, galti ho to maafi maangein, aur khushi mile to use saath celebrate karein.

Tumhari muskurahat bani rahe, tumhare sapne poore hon, tumhe zindagi mein woh sab mile jo tumhare liye behtar ho. Allah tumhe hamesha khush, mehfooz aur kamyaab rakhe.

Aur haan, aaj ka din sirf tumhara hai. Isliye aaj saari tension side mein rakhna aur khoob saara smile karna. You deserve beautiful moments, beautiful memories and all the happiness in the world. 🥺❤️
