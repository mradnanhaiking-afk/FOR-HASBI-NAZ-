Sabse pehle toh Happy Birthday meri pyaari Chugu. ❤️

Pata hai, bahut si galtiyan sabse hoti hain. Tumse bhi ho sakti hain, mujhse bhi hoti hain. Lekin tumhare alawa mere paas aur hai hi kaun jisse main apni har baat kahun? Kisse apni khushi baantun, kisse roothun aur kise manaun?

Main ye nahi kehta ke main hamesha sahi hota hoon. Kabhi meri baaton se tumhe bura lag sakta hai, kabhi main tumhari feelings samajhne mein der kar sakta hoon. Lekin yakeen karna, tumhari ahmiyat mere liye kabhi kam nahi hoti.

Agar kabhi humare beech koi galatfehmi aa jaaye, toh use humare rishte se bada mat hone dena. Gussa ho jaana, mujhse shikayat kar lena, lekin apne dil ki baat kehna kabhi band mat karna.

Mujhe tumhara har andaaz pyaara hai. Tumhara hansna, tumhara gussa karna, tumhara chhoti-chhoti baaton par khush ho jaana. Main chahta hoon tum mere saamne hamesha apni asli wali tum raho.

Main tumse perfection nahi chahta. Na hi chahta hoon ke tum har waqt khush rehne ka dikhawa karo. Udaas ho toh bata dena, pareshaan ho toh keh dena. Har baat akele sambhalna zaroori nahi hota.

Kabhi tumhe lage ke tumhari koi baat sunne wala nahi hai, toh mujhse keh dena. Ho sakta hai main har pareshani ka hal na de paaun, lekin tumhari baat ko samajhne ki poori koshish zaroor karunga.

Tumhari muskurahat mere liye bahut khaas hai. Isliye nahi ke tum hamesha muskurati raho, balki isliye ke tumhe muskurane ke liye kabhi apni khushi se samjhauta na karna pade.

Meri dil se dua hai ke Allah tumhari har mushkil aasaan kare, tumhare naseeb mein khushiyan likhe, tumhare sapnon ko haqeeqat banaye aur tumhe hamesha buri nazar aur har takleef se mehfooz rakhe.

Aur ek baat yaad rakhna: tumhe har kisi ke saamne khud ko saabit karne ki zaroorat nahi. Tum jaisi ho, apne aap mein bahut khoobsurat ho. Apni value kabhi mat bhoolna.

Main nahi jaanta ke aane wala waqt humare liye kya lekar aayega, lekin aaj main bas itna kehna chahta hoon: tum mere liye bahut special ho. Tumhari khushi, tumhari izzat aur tumhari feelings mere liye maayne rakhti hain.

Kabhi khud ko akela mat samajhna, Chugu. Apne sapne poore karna, khoob aage badhna, aur apni woh pyaari si smile kabhi mat khona.

Janamdin bahut bahut mubarak ho, meri pyaari Hasbi Naz. Hamesha khush raho, hamesha abaad raho. 🌷
